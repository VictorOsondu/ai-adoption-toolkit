## 2. Decision Rights

The programme owner may:

- coordinate the adoption roadmap and review rhythm
- convene the people needed for a decision
- maintain the shared programme tools and evidence
- pause an activity when an agreed stop condition is met
- escalate decisions outside their authority

The programme owner may not:

- approve tools, data use, employment decisions, or high-risk uses alone
- act as the organisation's legal, HR, security, procurement, or data-protection authority
- make champions responsible for unresolved governance questions
- declare value or safety without evidence

## 3. Core Responsibilities

- keep one prioritised roadmap
- connect executive direction, pilots, training, champions, governance, and measurement
- maintain clear routes for questions, tool requests, incidents, and escalation
- find useful internal examples and turn them into reusable guidance
- review adoption evidence and recommend continue / change / stop decisions
- keep leaders informed about value, risk, blockers, and decisions needed

## 5. Operating Rhythm

Weekly:

- review active pilots, questions, blockers, and incidents
- meet or respond to champions and pilot owners
- update decisions and next actions

Monthly:

- review adoption, value, risk, and support evidence
- update the roadmap and learning resources
- brief the executive sponsor

Quarterly:

- review outcomes, tool portfolio, policy, training coverage, and programme capacity
- decide what to scale, change, stop, or fund
