## 4. Stop Conditions

Stop or pause if:

- prohibited data enters the tool
- an incident or material near miss occurs
- required review is repeatedly bypassed
- the workflow affects people, rights, money, safety, or regulated activity beyond the approved scope
- the vendor, model, terms, or connected systems change materially
- participants feel pressured to continue despite credible concerns
