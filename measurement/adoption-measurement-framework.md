# AI Adoption Measurement Framework

Measure enough to guide decisions without turning AI adoption into prompt counting or employee surveillance.

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24. See [sources and verification notes](../sources.md).

## Three Layers

### 1. Reach

Use reach measures to learn whether approved support is reaching the intended people:

- relevant staff with approved access
- approved-tool use in the reporting period
- training or onboarding coverage by role
- teams with an active champion or support route

Reach is a starting condition, not proof of useful adoption.

### 2. Integration

Use integration measures to learn whether people are moving from isolated experiments to repeatable work:

- people using an approved tool on more than one day or for more than one cycle
- tested workflows repeated in real work
- occasional users becoming consistent users
- role-specific use cases documented and reused
- recurring blockers removed

Where aggregated product telemetry is available, define terms such as monthly active or engaged user before reporting them. Different tools count activity differently, so do not compare numbers without checking the definitions.

### 3. Outcomes

Use existing operational measures where possible:

- time or cycle-time change
- quality, rework, error, or verification effort
- customer, learner, client, or staff experience
- risk reduction, incidents, and near misses
- new capability or service improvement
- user confidence, pressure, and wellbeing
- where recovered time went

Measure the workflow before and after. Do not attribute every change to AI when tools, staffing, demand, or process changed at the same time.

## Measurement Rules

- Aggregate by team or programme where possible.
- Collect the minimum data needed for a named decision.
- Do not inspect prompt content merely to measure adoption.
- Do not rank individuals by prompt count, login frequency, or AI-generated output.
- Explain what telemetry is collected, why, who can see it, and how long it is retained.
- Pair quantitative signals with staff feedback and concrete workflow evidence.
- Check for pressure, exclusion, accessibility problems, and displaced work.
- Retire a measure when it no longer informs a decision.

## Avoid

- prompts, completions, or AI-touched documents presented as value
- licence assignment presented as adoption
- training attendance presented as competence
- self-reported time savings without a baseline or plausibility check
- precision that the available evidence cannot support

The purpose of measurement is to decide what to support, change, scale, or stop.
