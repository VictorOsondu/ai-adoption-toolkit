# Post-Rollout Staff Survey

A short anonymous survey after an AI pilot, training session, or rollout phase.

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24. See [sources and verification notes](../sources.md).

Use a 1-5 scale (1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree). Note that a high score on item 9 is a warning sign, not a good result — read it on its own rather than averaging it with the rest.

1. I understand what AI use is expected or encouraged here.
2. I know which tools are approved.
3. I know what data must stay out of public AI tools.
4. I have used AI for at least one real work task.
5. I know how to check AI output before relying on it.
6. The examples or training were relevant to my role.
7. I know how to report a concern, mistake, or near miss.
8. AI use has saved time or improved quality in at least one task.
9. AI use has created new confusion, pressure, or risk.
10. I know what support is available next.

## Open Questions

What was the most useful part of the rollout?

What still feels unclear or risky?

What should we stop, start, or change next?
