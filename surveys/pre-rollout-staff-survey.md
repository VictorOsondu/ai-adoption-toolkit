# Pre-Rollout Staff Survey

A short anonymous survey before an AI rollout or pilot. Adapt the wording to your setting.

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24. See [sources and verification notes](../sources.md).

Use a 1-5 scale (1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree). Note that a high score on item 7 is a warning sign, not a good result — read it on its own rather than averaging it with the rest.

1. I understand why we are exploring AI.
2. I know which AI tools I am allowed to use.
3. I know what information must not be entered into public AI tools.
4. I feel safe asking basic questions about AI.
5. I have seen at least one relevant example for my role.
6. I know who to ask when I am unsure.
7. I am concerned AI will be used in a way that creates risk or pressure.
8. I believe AI could help with at least one real task in my work.
9. I know how AI outputs should be checked before they are used.
10. I trust that mistakes or concerns can be raised without blame.

## Open Questions

What is one task where AI might help?

What is one concern you want addressed before rollout?

What support would make the first step easier?
