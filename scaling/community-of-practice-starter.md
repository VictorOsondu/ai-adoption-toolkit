## 3. Knowledge Flow

Use each channel for a different job:

1. **Conversation:** chat or meetings for quick questions, demos, and troubleshooting.
2. **Working record:** a discussion board or shared log for useful threads that need context and follow-up.
3. **Canonical guidance:** a wiki or approved-use-case library for stable, reviewed practice.

When a useful answer emerges:

- give it a clear title
- label it by role, task, tool, and risk where relevant
- record what was tested and what evidence exists
- have the right owner review it
- move mature guidance into the canonical library
- link back to the original discussion for context

## 4. Rhythm

Recurring session:

Suggested simple agenda:

1. One or two short demonstrations from real work.
2. Open questions and problem-solving.
3. Risks, uncertainties, or policy questions to escalate.
4. Useful material to document or update.

Between sessions:

- seed unanswered questions
- connect people solving similar problems
- correct unsafe or outdated guidance
- turn repeated questions into reusable material

## 5. Community Rules

- Use approved tools and follow data boundaries when demonstrating work.
- Redact client, customer, employee, pupil, patient, or other sensitive information.
- Separate tested practice from speculation.
- Do not pressure people to share prompts, usage data, or mistakes publicly.
- Move legal, HR, security, safeguarding, and incident questions to the proper route.

## 6. Health Check

Review:

- Are useful questions receiving answers?
- Are different roles participating?
- Are lessons becoming reusable guidance?
- Are risks and failures surfaced as well as wins?
- Is the community dependent on one person?
- Has activity produced a workflow, decision, or learning outcome?

Useful evidence:

- resolved questions
- approved use cases documented
- champion- or peer-led sessions
- repeated blockers removed
- examples of time, quality, service, or risk improvement

Membership or message volume alone does not demonstrate value.
