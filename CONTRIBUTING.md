# Contributing

The toolkit is the shared engine for the AI Adoption Playbooks, so contributions that make the instruments sharper, clearer, or more widely usable are welcome.

## What's welcome

- **Improvements to the tools** — a clearer question, a better scoring band, a fix to a confusing instruction.
- **Source updates** — changed public guidance, better references, or clearer evidence boundaries.
- **New instruments that fit the roadmap** — a survey, a monitoring template, a scaling aid. Open an issue first so we can agree scope before you build.
- **Field notes** — you used one of these with a real team and learned something about how it behaves. Tell us.
- **Translations** — faithful, credited.

## What isn't

- Vendor tools, affiliate links, or "add our product" submissions. The toolkit is vendor-neutral by design.
- Anything that turns a self-scored instrument into a compliance or certification claim — that layer is deliberately not here.
- Private client, organisational, personal, credential, regulated, security, or confidential information in public issues, PRs, examples, surveys, logs, or field reports.

## Standards

- Plain, specific, UK English. No hype, no "unlock", no "game-changer".
- Instruments must be usable by a non-technical person in one sitting, without a login or a purchase.
- Use public or synthetic examples unless the case is already public and permission is clear.
- Claims about AI literacy, compliance, data protection, adoption outcomes, or organisational practice should cite primary or high-authority sources where possible.
- Open an issue before large changes; PRs reviewed as promptly as a one-person team reasonably can.
