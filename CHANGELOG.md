# Changelog

This project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [0.1.0] — 2026-07-24

### Added
- Lean launch of the toolkit.
- **AI Adoption Readiness Assessment** — self-scored, twelve statements across four dimensions (direction, guardrails, capability, follow-through), with scoring bands and next steps.
- **Readiness Score Sheet** — dimension scoring, evidence check, and next-review prompt.
- **30-Day Adoption Action Plan** — focused improvement cycle after assessment.
- **AI Use Policy Check** — practical policy usability check.
- **Adoption Review Log** — monthly or post-pilot review.
- **Blame-Free AI Incident Log** — incident and near-miss learning template.
- **Pre- and Post-Rollout Staff Surveys** — lightweight staff confidence and concern checks.
- **Champion Cascade Plan** — scaling support through trusted peers.
- **AI Community of Practice Starter** — charter, channel roles, knowledge flow, operating rhythm, and health checks.
- **AI Programme Owner Charter** — mandate, decision rights, partnerships, capacity, cadence, and handover.
- **AI Pilot Brief** — baseline, controls, success measures, stop conditions, feedback, and final decision.
- **AI Onboarding Checklist** — consistent direction, tool, data, practice, and support onboarding.
- **Approved AI Use-Case Library** — reusable workflow records with evidence, boundaries, ownership, and review.
- **AI Adoption Measurement Framework** — reach, integration, and outcome measures with anti-surveillance safeguards.
- Expanded the Champion Cascade Plan with recruitment, protected time, onboarding, distributed ownership, and evidence.
- Sources and verification notes.
- Security guidance and issue templates for corrections and field testing.

### Notes
- Version starts at 0.x while the toolkit is field-tested and refined. It reaches 1.0.0 after named field use or a documented pilot validates the core workflow.
