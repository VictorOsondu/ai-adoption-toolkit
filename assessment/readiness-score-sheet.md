## Lowest-Score Rule

Start with the lowest-scoring dimension unless there is a live safety, data, legal, safeguarding, or client-risk issue. Risk issues come first.

## Evidence Check

For every score of `2`, write down the proof. If there is no proof, score it `1`.

Examples of proof:

- named accountable owner
- written AI purpose statement
- current AI use policy
- training record
- approved tool list
- incident-report route
- adoption review notes
- onboarding checklist
